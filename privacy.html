Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Chrome extension Copy With Format (“the Extension”). It describes what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and whether we share it with third parties.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Extension, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: If you sign in on our official website, the Extension stores your login token and basic user profile locally so the Extension can stay signed in.
  • Extension settings: Preferences such as whether the Extension is enabled, language, and related options are saved in Chrome local storage on your device.
  • Copied or saved content: If you use history or cloud-sync features, the text or formatted content you choose to save may be sent to our servers so you can view it later.
  • Technical data required for operation: The Extension uses Chrome permissions (activeTab, tabs, storage) to detect the current page, inject copy buttons on supported AI websites, and remember settings. We do not use these permissions to browse your other websites in the background.

We do not intentionally collect government ID numbers, payment card numbers, or precise geolocation through the Extension.

2. How we use the information

  • Provide copy-with-format, export, login, and history features.
  • Keep you signed in and restore your settings on this browser.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Extension and related website services.
  • Detect abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not sell user data. We do not use Extension user data for unrelated third-party advertising.

3. How we store and handle the information

  • Settings, login token, and local preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.
  • If you use account or history features, related data is stored on our servers associated with copywithformat.com.
  • You can remove locally stored Extension data by removing the Extension from Chrome, or by signing out where that option is available.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: Infrastructure needed to run the official website and APIs (for example cloud hosting). These providers process data only to provide the service.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • User-initiated actions: If you copy or export content into another app (for example Word, a clipboard manager, or an AI website), that destination is outside this Extension and follows that product’s own policy.

The Extension injects scripts only on supported AI chat websites to add copy and export UI. Those websites are not our products; we do not sell your chat content to them through the Extension.

5. Permissions

  • storage: save settings and login state on your device.
  • tabs / activeTab: open help or official pages, and detect whether the current tab is a supported AI chat page.
  • Host access on listed AI sites: show the copy-with-format button and read the answer content you choose to copy or export.

6. Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the Extension after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support page or visit https://www.copywithformat.com/.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 20 日

本政策适用于 Chrome 扩展程序 Copy With Format（带格式复制），说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用、如何存储，以及是否与第三方共享。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息：若你在官网登录，扩展会在本地保存登录 token 和基本用户资料，以便保持登录状态。
  • 扩展设置：如是否启用插件、语言等，保存在你设备上的 Chrome 本地存储中。
  • 复制或保存的内容：若你使用历史记录或云同步功能，你选择保存的文本或带格式内容可能被发送到我们的服务器，供你之后查看。
  • 运行所需的技术信息：扩展使用 activeTab、tabs、storage 权限，用于识别当前页面、在支持的 AI 网站插入复制按钮，以及记住设置。我们不会用这些权限在后台浏览你的其他网站。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 提供带格式复制、导出、登录和历史记录功能。
  • 在本浏览器中保持登录并恢复设置。
  • 运营、维护和改进扩展及相关网站服务。
  • 识别滥用行为并保障服务安全。

我们不会出售用户数据，也不会将扩展中的用户数据用于无关的第三方广告。

3. 存储与处理

  • 设置、登录 token 和本地偏好保存在设备上的 chrome.storage.local。
  • 若使用账号或历史记录功能，相关数据会存储在与 copywithformat.com 关联的服务器上。
  • 卸载扩展，或在提供退出登录的入口退出后，可清除本地存储的扩展数据。

4. 第三方共享

我们不会出售或出租你的个人信息。仅在以下情况可能共享：

  • 为运行官网和接口而使用的云主机等服务商，且仅用于提供服务；
  • 法律法规或合法程序要求；
  • 你主动把内容复制或导出到其他应用时，由该应用自行适用其隐私政策。

5. 联系我们

隐私相关问题请通过 Support 页面联系，或访问官网 https://www.copywithformat.com/。
