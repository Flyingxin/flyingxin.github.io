Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Chrome extension Copy With Format (“the Extension”). It describes what information we process, how we use it, how it is stored, and whether we share it with third parties.

The Extension stores data only on this device, in your local browser. Copied content, settings, and related data stay on your computer. They are not uploaded to our servers, not synced to the cloud, and are not used in a way that would leak your information.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Extension, the following information may be processed locally in your browser:

  • Extension settings: Preferences such as whether the Extension is enabled, language, and related options are saved in Chrome local storage on your device.
  • Copied or exported content: When you copy or export an answer, the content is read and processed in the current browser tab on your device so the Extension can keep formatting and generate Word, PDF, Markdown, or image files. This content is not sent to our servers.
  • Technical data required for operation: The Extension uses the Chrome storage permission to remember settings on this device. We do not use this permission to browse your other websites in the background.

We do not collect government ID numbers, payment card numbers, or precise geolocation through the Extension. We do not operate a login or cloud-save feature at this time, so we do not receive your account data or copied content on our servers.

2. How we use the information

  • Provide copy-with-format and export features on your device.
  • Restore your settings on this browser from local storage.
  • Operate and improve the Extension’s local features.

We do not sell user data. We do not use Extension user data for advertising. Because data stays in your browser, there is no server-side profile of your copied content.

3. How we store and handle the information

  • Settings and local preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device. This storage is limited to this browser on this computer.
  • Copied content is handled in memory in the current page or export flow. It is not uploaded, not stored in our cloud, and not shared with us.
  • You can remove locally stored Extension data by removing the Extension from Chrome, or by clearing the Extension’s site/storage data in Chrome settings.

No login, account, or cloud-sync service is enabled in the current version. Your data does not leave your browser for storage on our side.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or upload your personal information or copied content. We do not share Extension data with third parties for their own use. Limited cases:

  • Legal requirements: If required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process. The current Extension does not send your copied content or settings to our servers, so we do not hold that data to disclose.
  • User-initiated actions: If you copy or export content into another app (for example Word, a clipboard manager, or an AI website), that destination is outside this Extension and follows that product’s own policy.

The Extension injects scripts only on supported AI chat websites to add copy and export UI. Those websites are not our products; we do not send your chat content to our servers, and we do not sell your chat content through the Extension.

5. Permissions

  • storage: save settings on your device only. Data stays in local browser storage and is not synced to our cloud.

6. Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the Extension after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support page or visit https://test.copywithformat.com/.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

本政策适用于 Chrome 扩展程序 Copy With Format（带格式复制），说明我们处理哪些信息、如何使用、如何存储，以及是否与第三方共享。

本扩展的数据完全由本机浏览器存储。复制内容、设置及相关数据仅保存在你的这台电脑、这个浏览器中，不会上传到我们的服务器，不会进行云端同步，也不涉及因云端保存而导致的信息泄露。

1. 我们收集的信息

根据你的使用方式，以下信息可能在本机浏览器内被处理：

  • 扩展设置：如是否启用插件、语言等，保存在你设备上的 Chrome 本地存储中。
  • 复制或导出的内容：当你复制或导出回答时，内容仅在当前浏览器标签页中读取和处理，用于保留格式并生成 Word、PDF、Markdown 或图片。这些内容不会发送到我们的服务器。
  • 运行所需的技术信息：扩展仅使用 Chrome 的 storage 权限，用于在本机记住设置。我们不会用该权限在后台浏览你的其他网站。

我们不会通过扩展收集证件号、支付卡号或精确地理位置。当前版本不提供登录或云端保存功能，因此我们不会在服务器上接收你的账号数据或复制内容。

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 在本机提供带格式复制和导出功能。
  • 从本机存储恢复你在此浏览器中的设置。
  • 运营和改进扩展的本地功能。

我们不会出售用户数据，也不会将扩展中的用户数据用于广告。由于数据只存在本机浏览器，我们不会在服务器上建立你的复制内容档案。

3. 存储与处理

  • 设置和本地偏好保存在设备上的 chrome.storage.local，仅限于这台电脑上的这个浏览器。
  • 复制内容仅在当前页面或导出流程的内存中处理，不会上传、不会存入我们的云端，也不会共享给我们。
  • 卸载扩展，或在 Chrome 设置中清除该扩展的存储数据，即可删除本机保存的扩展数据。

当前版本未启用登录、账号或云同步服务。你的数据不会离开浏览器、也不会存储到我们一侧。

4. 第三方共享

我们不会出售、出租或上传你的个人信息或复制内容，也不会把扩展数据提供给第三方用于其自身用途。仅在以下情况可能涉及：

  • 法律法规或合法程序要求。当前扩展不会把你的复制内容或设置发送到我们的服务器，因此我们并不持有这类数据可供披露；
  • 你主动把内容复制或导出到其他应用时，由该应用自行适用其隐私政策。

5. 权限说明

  • storage：仅用于在本机保存设置。数据留在浏览器本地存储中，不会同步到我们的云端。

6. 联系我们

隐私相关问题请通过 Support 页面联系，或访问官网 https://test.copywithformat.com/。
